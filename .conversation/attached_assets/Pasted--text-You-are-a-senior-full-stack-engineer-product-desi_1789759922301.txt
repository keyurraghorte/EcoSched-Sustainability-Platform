This is a functional academic/engineering project, not merely a static UI prototype.

============================================================
2. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
============================================================

EcoSched is a carbon-aware cloud workload scheduling platform.

The system analyzes cloud workloads together with data-center energy availability, renewable-energy sources, weather conditions, workload deadlines, and energy requirements.

The system should compare:

BEFORE:
Conventional/baseline workload scheduling

AFTER:
EcoSched energy- and carbon-aware scheduling

The final application should show whether EcoSched changes:
- Energy consumption
- Renewable-energy utilization
- Grid-energy usage
- Estimated CO₂ emissions
- Workload completion/deadline performance
- Energy efficiency

The application must clearly distinguish real API data, database data, calculated data, simulated data, and demo data.

============================================================
3. PROBLEM STATEMENT
============================================================

Cloud data centers consume significant amounts of electricity.

When workloads are scheduled without considering renewable-energy availability, weather conditions, energy sources, and carbon impact, workloads may use more grid electricity than necessary.

EcoSched demonstrates an intelligent scheduling approach that considers available renewable energy and workload constraints while comparing its results with conventional scheduling.

------------------------------------------------------------
SEGMENT TREE
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
Efficiently represent/query/update relevant scheduling or resource-availability information over time.

Inputs:
- Time intervals
- Resource/energy availability
- Workload scheduling requirements
- Relevant scheduling constraints

Processing:
- Build the required tree structure
- Support efficient interval/range queries
- Support required updates as scheduling progresses
- Use the results in the EcoSched scheduling process

Output:
Efficient availability/resource information used by the scheduler.

------------------------------------------------------------
DECREASING BIN-PACKING HEURISTIC
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
Efficiently assign workloads/resources while considering capacity and energy-related constraints.

Processing:
- Analyze workload requirements
- Order workloads according to the defined decreasing heuristic
- Assign workloads to appropriate scheduling/resource slots
- Respect capacity and deadline constraints
- Consider renewable-energy availability

Do not remove mandatory constraints.
